FAQ

Common questions

What is mobile auto detailing?

We bring everything we need to your driveway, apartment, or office — water, power, lights, products, the works. You don't have to drop your car off or wait in a lobby. Book a time, hand off the keys, and get a freshly detailed car a couple of hours later.

What areas do you service?

Crystal Whip is based in Cedar Park, TX and serves Austin, Brushy Creek, Georgetown, Round Rock, and Pflugerville. If you're nearby and not sure, give us a call.

Do you need anything from me?

Just access to your vehicle and a spot to park it. We carry our own power and water for apartments and offices, and we'll let you know if there's anything specific we need before the appointment.

How long does a detail take?

Most exterior + interior packages take 1.5–3 hours. Ceramic coatings and full paint correction are full-day jobs. We'll give you a clear ETA when you book.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, card, and all major mobile pay options. Payment is collected after the work is done and you're happy with the result.

Do I need to be home the whole time?

Not at all. Most clients meet us at the start, hand over the keys, and head back to their day. We'll text you when the car is finished.
